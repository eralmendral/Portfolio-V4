Eric Almendral Logo Asset Pack

Included:
- 00-clean-base: clean full logo and logo mark on white background
- 01-dark-mode: white logo on dark background, plus white transparent PNGs
- 02-logo-mark-no-name: logo mark only, no name
- 03-transparent: full and mark-only transparent PNGs in black and white
- 04-favicons: favicon PNG sizes, favicon.ico, and apple-touch-icon
- 05-pwa-icons: standard and maskable PWA PNG icons plus a starter manifest.webmanifest

Notes:
- PWA manifest paths are relative placeholders. Move icons into your app's public icons folder and adjust `src` paths as needed.
- Favicon and PWA icons use the logo mark only so they remain readable at small sizes.
